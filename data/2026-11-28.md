Result: 该态的ρ^{Γ}非正（NPT），因而纠缠；但任意数量的拷贝都无法通过LOCC协议提纯出纠缠；即使允许更一般的可分离操作，输出两比特与Bell态的保真度也不超过(1+√2)/4≈0.604。由此得到可提纯纠缠既不满足可加性也不满足凸性。

Conclusion: 存在NPT纠缠态在LOCC及可分离操作下均不可提纯，故可提纯纠缠不具备可加性与凸性，打破了此前关于其良好性质的普遍预期，对纠缠度量理论具有基础性意义。

Abstract: We show that the Werner state $ρ= (23 I - 3\,\mathrm{SWAP})/1448$ on $\mathbb{C}^8 \otimes \mathbb{C}^8$ has a negative partial transpose and is bound entangled: no LOCC protocol can distill entanglement from any number of copies of $ρ$, and even with separable operations the fidelity of a two-qubit output with a Bell state never exceeds $(1+\sqrt{2})/4 \approx 0.604$. It follows that distillable entanglement is neither additive nor convex.
