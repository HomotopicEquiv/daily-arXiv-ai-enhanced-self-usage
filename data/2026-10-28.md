### [1] [Quantum Evolution Kernel for Node-Attributed Graphs](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.36984)
*Manuel Padovani,Alessandra Di Pierro,Claudia Daffara*

Main category: quant-ph

TL;DR: 提出将节点属性图嵌入中性原子量子计算机的方法，扩展QEK引入基于三能级量子态的节点级特征编码，并用于数字识别任务。


<details>
  <summary>Details</summary>
Motivation: 现有PASQAL量子演化核（QEK）缺乏对节点属性特征的编码能力，需一种能将节点属性融入图嵌入的量子方案，并探索其在分类任务中的潜力。

Method: 以三能级（qutrit）量子态实现节点级特征编码，扩展QEK形成图嵌入框架；用Pulser库对全量子过程进行模拟；基于嵌入提取相关性信息并采用Wasserstein距离进行分类。

Result: 在数字识别任务上，采用Wasserstein距离的方法平均准确率高于常用的Jensen-Shannon距离方法；模拟受Pulser限制导致实验数据规模受限，并与经典基线进行了对比。

Conclusion: 该原型验证了中性原子量子计算机上节点属性图嵌入的可行性与分类优势，为后续扩展与实际硬件验证提供依据。

Abstract: We present a novel approach to embed node-attributed graphs onto neutral-atom quantum computers, and propose an application to digit recognition. Our approach extends PASQAL's quantum evolution kernel (QEK) by introducing a node-level feature encoding based on three-level quantum states. To this purpose, we present a prototype showing how the resulting embedding can be leveraged to extract correlation-based information for classification tasks. The entire quantum process is simulated using Pasqal's Pulser library, which inevitably imposes constraints on the size of the experimental datasets. We compare our method with classical baselines to evaluate its potential. We show that, for the digit recognition task, our method achieves a higher mean accuracy than the more commonly used Jensen-Shannon distance-based methods by utilizing the Wasserstein distance.
